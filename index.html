YOUR QUICK START GUIDE

How to play

New here? Follow these steps to get Johnny flying.

1
Start the game

Tap the green Start game button. It may take a few seconds to load.

2
Choose English

When the language list appears, press SELECT / OK.

3
Go through the menus

Use the arrows to scroll. Press SELECT / OK for the option shown on the bottom left of the game.

4
Read the tutorial

At “Welcome to Texas”, press the down arrow several times to scroll through the instructions and close them.

5
Launch Johnny!

Press LAUNCH / FLY to fire. Hold it during flight to flap his arms.
